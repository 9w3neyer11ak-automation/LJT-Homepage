This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for chart understanding. Junteng Liu is the first author. The paper is co-authored by Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. The code is available at [Vision4Chart](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Vision4Chart).
